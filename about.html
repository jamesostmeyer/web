About Mythic Robotics

Mythic Robotics educates and inspires young people through affordable, secular, and inclusive hands-on experiences in science, technology, engineering, the arts, and mathematics.

Our Work

Our primary activities include robotics teams and competitions, technical workshops, mentoring, collaborative projects, and other educational programs that develop creativity, technical skills, collaboration, character, and a lifelong love of learning.

Our History

Mythic Robotics was founded in 2026 to prepare for its rookie season in the FIRST Robotics Competition in 2027.
