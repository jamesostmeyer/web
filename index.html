Mythic Robotics

Mythic Robotics educates and inspires young people through affordable, secular, and inclusive hands-on experiences in science, technology, engineering, the arts, and mathematics.

Our programs include MYTHIC 11817, a FIRST Robotics Competition team, and FIRST LEGO League teams for elementary and middle school students.

Mythic Robotics is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN: 42-4319954) that operates FIRST® Robotics Competition (FRC) team MYTHIC 11817 in the greater Kansas City area.
